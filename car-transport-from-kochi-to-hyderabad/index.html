About this route

The Kochi to Hyderabad route is a medium-distance route with frequent truck departures. Our trucks typically travel via NH 66 and NH 544 out of Kerala, then NH 44 and NH 65 into Telangana, with rest stops at secure, monitored yards along the way. You get WhatsApp updates at pickup, loading, each major checkpoint and delivery.

Your car's journey, step by step

  1. Book Booking confirmed, pickup slot fixed, documents checked.
  2. Pickup Doorstep pickup in Kochi, photo inspection, loading on carrier.
  3. On the road Travelling via Kerala and Telangana, with WhatsApp updates.
  4. Delivery Unloading and doorstep delivery in Hyderabad, joint inspection and handover.

Frequently asked questions

What is the car transport cost from Kochi to Hyderabad?

It depends on your car size and the care option. We give one fixed, locked price for your car, with tolls, fuel and insurance included. Send the instant quote and we share it on WhatsApp in minutes.

When will my car reach Hyderabad?

We share the expected delivery date when you book, and you get live GPS updates on WhatsApp all along the 1,120 km journey.

Is car transport from Kochi to Hyderabad door-to-door?

Yes. We collect your car from your address in Kochi and deliver it to your address in Hyderabad.

What documents do I need to move my car from Kochi to Hyderabad?

A copy of the RC, valid insurance and your ID proof. We guide you on any interstate paperwork for this route.
