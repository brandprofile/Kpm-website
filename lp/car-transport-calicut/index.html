FAQs

How does KPM transport my car?

Your car travels on a dedicated car-carrier truck, not on its own wheels. We pick it up from your door, load it on the truck with wheel locks and straps, and drive it to your destination where we unload and hand it over at your door.

How much does car transport cost in India?

Price depends on distance, car size and the care option (standard or premium). Our instant quote shows an estimate for your exact route in seconds, and every city and route page lists typical prices. The price we confirm is the price you pay, with no hidden charges.

When will my car be delivered?

You get the expected delivery date when you book, and live GPS updates on WhatsApp all the way to your door.

Is my car insured during transit?

Yes. Every shipment is covered by transit insurance for the declared value of the car. We record the car's condition with photos at pickup and delivery so any claim is simple.

Are your car carriers open or closed?

Every KPM truck is fully closed. The side panels open only for loading and close completely for the journey, so your car is covered from dust, rain and stones all the way. For luxury, new and vintage cars you can add Premium care with a top-deck spot and soft straps.

Do you pick up from my home?

Yes, we offer true door-to-door service. Our driver collects the car from your home, office or showroom and delivers it to the address you give, in cities, towns and most villages across India.
